Together we can connect hearts, transform lives, and foster a sense of belonging for everyone.

The Community Innovation Center (CIC) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering individuals and transforming communities through innovative solutions. Based on the southwest side of Chicago, we focus on fostering growth and creating opportunities by addressing key areas such as entrepreneurship, civic engagement, wellness, technology, food insecurity, and the arts. Our programs are designed to equip individuals, businesses, and organizations with the tools they need to succeed while promoting collaboration and inclusivity.

At CIC, we believe that innovation is the key to sustainable progress. By connecting passionate people with resources and ideas, we aim to tackle pressing challenges and inspire positive change. From supporting local entrepreneurs to bridging the digital divide, our initiatives are rooted in a commitment to building stronger, healthier, and more vibrant communities.
