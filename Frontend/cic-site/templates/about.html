Our story

Turning ideas into action on Chicago's southwest side

The Community Innovation Center (CIC) is a nonprofit that helps individuals, businesses, and organizations turn good ideas into real results. Through hands-on programs and local partnerships, we work across six areas: entrepreneurship, civic engagement, wellness, technology, food insecurity, and the arts.

Our work shows up in everyday ways, from a neighborhood food pantry to snow removal for seniors.

Toys and Coats Drive

Every year, donors, volunteers, and local partners help us collect toys and coats for neighbors in need. In 2024 the drive went beyond our goals and brought winter clothing and toys to hundreds of families.

What we do

Core initiatives

Six focus areas guide our programs and partnerships.

💼

Entrepreneurship

A small business growth accelerator with mentorship, funding leads, and shared workspace, plus financial literacy workshops and networking nights with investors and industry experts.

🗳

Civic Engagement

Community forums on local issues, voter education on rights and the voting process, and volunteer matching that connects residents with service projects.

🌿

Health & Wellness

Free yoga and mindfulness classes, mobile health clinics for underserved areas, and mental health awareness with stress workshops and lower-cost counseling through local partners.

💻

Technology

Beginner coding bootcamps for youth and adults, digital literacy training, and a tech lending library with laptops and Wi-Fi hotspots for families in need.

🎨

Art

Community exhibitions featuring local artists, classes in painting, music, and theater for all ages, and Community Performance Nights.

🍎

Food Insecurity

Expanded food distribution with fresh produce and pantry staples, budget-friendly cooking workshops, and partnerships with local farmers.
